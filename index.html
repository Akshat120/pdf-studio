Studio v1.0
The free & easy way to work with PDFs online!

Tools

Did you know? Your files never leave your computer. Everything happens right here in your browser — no uploads, no sign-up!

Merge PDFs

Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose. Rotate a file with ↺ / ↻ to turn all of its pages.

Drag files to change the order — or focus one and press Shift + ← / →. Files are merged left to right, top to bottom.

Add two or more PDFs to get started.

Organize pages

Drag pages to reorder. Rotate, delete, or pull out just the pages you need.

Click a page to select it. Drag to reorder — or focus a page and press Shift + ← / →.

Add text & images

Click on the page where you want to place something, then press “Add to page”.

Fit width

Standard fonts support Latin characters only.

72 points = 1 inch. The image's top-left corner goes where you click.

Position: click on the page

Watermark

Stamp text across the pages of a PDF.

Choose a PDF above to begin.

Fill a form

Load a PDF with fillable fields, type in the values, and save.

Images to PDF

Turn photos or scans (JPG, PNG, HEIC, WebP…) into a PDF, one image per page.

Drag images to change the order — or focus one and press Shift + ← / →. Each image becomes one page, left to right, top to bottom. Click an image to view it full size.

Add one or more images to get started.

Remove password

Unlock a password-protected PDF, or remove restrictions on printing, copying and editing, so it opens freely everywhere.

Only unlock PDFs you own or have permission to open. Your file and password never leave your device.

Choose a PDF above to begin.

Lock PDF

Protect a PDF with a password, and optionally stop others printing, copying or editing it. Uses AES-256, the strongest PDF encryption.

Permissions password (optional)

Only needed if you want to lift the restrictions later in a PDF editor. If you leave it empty, a strong random one is used.

· Keep your password safe — a forgotten password can’t be recovered. Nothing is uploaded.

Choose a PDF above to begin.

Redact PDF

Black out names, numbers, signatures or anything else — for good. Drag boxes over what to hide, or find text to mark it everywhere.

Fit width

Drag across the page to mark an area. Click a box’s × to remove it.

Finds every match on every page (not case-sensitive).

No areas marked yet.

Pages with boxes are turned into images, so the hidden text is really gone — not just covered. Their other text can’t be selected afterwards. Pages without boxes stay as they are. Bookmarks, form fields and document info are removed.

Compress PDF

Make a PDF smaller to email or upload. The biggest savings come from photos and scans — text-only PDFs are usually compact already.

Compression level
Choose a PDF above to begin.

Compress images

Shrink photos and pictures from iPhone, Android or the web — HEIC, HEIF, JPEG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, GIF and BMP.

Location and camera data (EXIF) are removed. Animated GIFs become still images. Browsers can’t save HEIC, so iPhone photos are saved as JPEG unless you pick another format.

Add images to get started.

Resize photo

Set an exact size in pixels, mm, cm or inches — or pick a passport, visa or social media size and position the crop.

Drag the box to choose what’s kept. For passport photos, line the face up with the dashed oval.

✔ Resized

Title & info

Change a PDF’s title — the name shown in the browser tab and reader’s title bar — and its other properties.
